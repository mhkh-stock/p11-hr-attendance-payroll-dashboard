# P11 — Package Note (Fiverr-ready)

**Gig title:** I will build your HR attendance and payroll Excel dashboard with live formulas

**What the client gets:** not a typed-up salary sheet — one workbook
where attendance drives payroll. The owner pastes the month's
attendance and the salary list, and the workbook counts every
present / late / half-day / leave / absent day per employee,
applies the company's deduction rules, EOBI and tax, and produces
a net-pay column plus a dashboard that names the top absentee, the
most-late employee and the weakest department — all by formula, so
next month is a data paste, not a rebuild. The client stops doing
payroll arithmetic by hand and gets one page that says who showed
up, who didn't, and what everyone is owed.

## Tiers (Hashir's band — $65/$140 tier-led)

| Tier | Price | Delivery | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | $20 | 2 days | Attendance summary workbook only (per-employee counts + attendance % + dashboard KPIs) from the client's attendance register, Excel |
| Standard | $65 | 4 days | Full workbook: Dashboard + Attendance + Payroll (overtime, late/absence deductions, EOBI, tax, net pay — all formula-driven) built on the client's staff list and attendance, 1 revision round |
| Premium | $140 | 6 days | Full workbook + the client's own deduction/tax rules fitted to their policy + a reusable monthly template (paste next month's register, payroll rebuilds itself) + a 1-page findings note naming the month's attendance risks, 2 revision rounds |

**What's NOT included:** the client's own attendance register and
salary list — the client sends them (register export, photo or
sheet); statutory filing or FBR/EOBI submissions (the workbook
computes the amounts, filing stays with the client's accountant);
multi-month history merge (quoted separately).

**Delivery time note:** counts from the day the client uploads their
attendance register and staff list, not from order placement.
